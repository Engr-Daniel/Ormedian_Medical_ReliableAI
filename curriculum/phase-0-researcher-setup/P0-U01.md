# P0-U01 — Researcher Mindset & Scientific Workflow

## Purpose

Develop the habits of mind needed to investigate technical and scientific problems systematically before moving into model implementation.

## Core learning outcomes

By the end of this unit, the trainee should be able to:

- distinguish learning, engineering, experimentation, and research;
- distinguish a result from a research contribution;
- connect claims to evidence;
- separate observation from interpretation;
- formulate a specific research question and falsifiable hypothesis;
- explain why experiments should answer questions;
- interpret informative negative results appropriately;
- draw conclusions that remain within the scope of the evidence;
- describe research as an iterative process.

## Learning sequence

1. Study the P0-U01 lecture note.
2. Complete the recommended reading selectively.
3. Complete the guided reasoning lab.
4. Prepare a short teach-back slide deck in your own words.
5. Complete P0-A01 independently.
6. Review the assessment with the mentor.
7. Record a short reflection in `research-notes/`.
