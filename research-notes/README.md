# Research notes

Keep human reasoning here: dated journals, reading notes, questions, diagnostic reasoning, and reflections. `research-notes/` contains thinking; [experiments/](../experiments/README.md) contains execution evidence.

Use descriptive filenames that include the relevant unit or experiment ID and, for journal entries, the date. In each entry, record what you learned or attempted, what failed, the evidence observed, your interpretation, uncertain assumptions, and the next question or check. Preserve useful negative results.

Link related execution records and literature citation keys instead of copying logs or paper PDFs. Exclude credentials and identifiable patient information. Agent-maintained [project memory](../memory.md) records project decisions; it does not replace a learner's research journal.

Phase 0 deliverables are listed in the [phase guide](../curriculum/phase-0-researcher-setup/README.md). Templates and learner entries will be added with the units.
